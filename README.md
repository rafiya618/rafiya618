## About

I am a **Backend Focused Developer** with a focus on **AI and Full Stack Development**.

I have a background in Computer Science and enjoy building applications where the backend, databases, APIs, and AI features work together. I have worked with Node.js, Python, Java, React, Next.js, and several databases including MongoDB, PostgreSQL, MySQL, and Redis.

I have built REST APIs, authentication systems, background jobs, real time features, AI chatbots, RAG applications, and full stack products.

When building applications, I prefer **clean, modular, and maintainable code**. I keep different parts of an application separated by their responsibilities and use services, reusable modules, queues, and background workers when they are useful for the project.

I also contribute to open source projects and enjoy learning by working with real code and real applications.
